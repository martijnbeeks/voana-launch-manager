## Creative QA — defects launched knowingly

All four batches were reviewed image-by-image before launch. The defects below were shown to
Martijn, who chose "launch all four as-is". Recording them here because they are **baked into
the images** and can only be cleared by a re-render.

### #565 — AI-garbled product labels (3 of 4 statics)
- `1.png`: "SONCHRE" (should be SKINCARE), "SLIM FELO PROTEETION" (SKIN FOLD PROTECTION),
  three gibberish icon captions.
- `4.png`: **"ANTI-ERICTION STICK"** — legible without zooming, worst of the set.
- `2.png`: main lines clean; icon captions read "Geotle Formula", "1oo Recidue", "Nott-Greasy".
- `3.png`: minor icon-caption defects.
- The sheet's own breakthrough memo rates this batch **CANNOT DEPLOY without re-render** and
  recommends freezing AI product-visual generation until photographed-label compositing exists.

### #570 — copy defects (labels clean; statics are text-only)
- `3.png`: **"sundrss"** typo (should be "sundress").
- `2.png`: "prescription stuff from the GP" — UK phrasing in a US-only ad set.
- No "based on real customer experiences" disclaimer, and the three transformation details
  reuse specifics from the prior named-testimonial batches (Diane K. / Catherine W. / Sandra P.).
  Either documented repurposing or constructed narrative — unresolved.

### #584
- `2.png`: Voana label garbles to "AXAL FRICTIOS CDC…"; the first word is ambiguous between
  "ANTI" and a reading that should not appear on a live ad. Small and blurred in frame, but real.
- `1.png`: the Lotrimin AF tube is **AI-fabricated** — brand marks approximate, side text gibberish.
  Depicting a competitor's trademarked packaging as an invented render is its own exposure.
- `3.png`: "Ales Vera" (Aloe Vera) plus garbled icon captions.

### #585
- `2.png`: "ANTL PRICTION STICX"; the Desitin tube is **mirrored** (text runs backwards).
- `3.png`: the whole photo is horizontally flipped — Gold Bond, Desitin, Lotrimin AF and A+D
  all read backwards.
- `1.png`: "ASK ANY NURSE" asserts professional consensus; needs supporting evidence.

### Cross-batch
- **Named-brand comparative claims** (Gold Bond, Desitin, Lotrimin AF, A+D) across #584/#585
  remain without the legal review the memos have flagged for six consecutive batches
  (UK ASA CAP Code 3.33 / FTC 16 CFR Part 14). #585 `3.png`'s trash-can framing adds a
  disparagement question beyond standard comparative review.
- **Rating conflict**: #584/#585 images and copy say **4.8/5**; #565 and CLAUDE.md say **4.9/5**.
  Martijn chose "match each image", so primary text follows the image in each batch. The true
  current rating is still unconfirmed and one of the two is wrong account-wide.
- **File numbering ≠ brief numbering** again (#565 `1.png` is the brief's Image 4; #585 files
  1–3 are the brief's Ads 7–9). Ads are named by file number, consistent with prior batches.
- No `instagram_user_id` set — same as every prior batch; ads deliver on Facebook surfaces.
